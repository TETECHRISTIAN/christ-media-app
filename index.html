CHANGEMENT DE FOND **Rôle : Master en écriture de prompt IA, 20 ans d'expérience** ``` TASK: Background replacement only. Preserve the subject(s) from the reference photo with 100% fidelity — do not alter, regenerate, or reinterpret their faces, facial features, skin texture, body proportions, pose, clothing, clothing textures, or the original framing/crop of the photo. Treat the subject layer as a locked, untouched element. SUBJECT (from reference image — keep exactly as is): The subject(s) exactly as they appear in the uploaded reference photo, same facial identity, same expression, same body posture, same outfit and fabric texture, same lighting direction on the skin and clothes as originally photographed. No beautification, no reshaping, no age or skin tone changes, no smoothing filters. NEW BACKGROUND (from reference backdrop image): [Describe the desired backdrop here — e.g. floral event backdrop, traditional wedding decor, studio, outdoor garden, church interior] LIGHTING & INTEGRATION: Match the color temperature and light direction of the new background to the existing lighting on the subject so the composite looks like it was shot in one take — warm ambient tone, soft diffused key light, natural contact shadows at the subject's feet grounding them on the backdrop's floor. CAMERA / OUTPUT: Same camera angle, same focal length/perspective, and same aspect ratio as the original reference photo. Photorealistic, high resolution, sharp focus on the subject, natural depth of field with the background slightly softer than the subject to create realistic separation. STRICT CONSTRAINTS: - Do not change the subject's identity, facial structure, or body shape in any way. - Do not crop or re-frame the subject differently than the original. - Do not add objects, props, or people not present in the reference. - Do not stylize the subject to match the background aesthetic. - Output must look like a real, single photograph, not a visible composite. ``` ### Variantes de fond prêtes à l'emploi - **Mariage traditionnel togolais/africain** : tissus wax orange/or/rouge, raffia, feuilles de palmier, calebasses, tapis tressé. - **Studio uni professionnel** : fond gris/blanc/bleu clair sans texture, vignette douce, éclairage studio homogène. - **Extérieur nature** : jardin luxuriant, lumière dorée, bokeh naturel, chemin flouté. - **Intérieur église / cérémonie** : bancs en bois flous, vitraux, décor floral blanc et or, ambiance solennelle. *(Détails complets de chaque variante disponibles dans l'historique de conversation — à redemander si besoin.)* --- ## 2. EXPOSITION, BALANCE DES BLANCS & AMBIANCE **Rôle : Directeur de la photographie, 20 ans d'expérience sur des productions américaines** ``` ROLE: You are acting as a professional Director of Photography with 20+ years of experience on major feature films, specializing in naturalistic lighting, color grading, and photorealistic exposure correction. TASK: Apply a full photographic and cinematographic correction pass to the generated image WITHOUT altering the subject's identity, facial features, pose, clothing, or composition. This is a grading/exposure pass only — not a content or subject edit. EXPOSURE: - Correct any over-exposed highlights (especially on skin, white fabrics, and light sources) to retain natural detail and texture — no blown-out whites. - Correct any under-exposed shadows to retain visible detail without crushing blacks, unless deep shadow is intentional to the mood. - Balance the overall dynamic range so both highlights and shadows hold detail, similar to a properly metered digital cinema camera. - Ensure the subject's face is the correctly exposed focal point — neither washed out nor underlit relative to the background. WHITE BALANCE: - Correct the white balance so skin tones read as natural and true-to-life — avoid unwanted orange/yellow cast or unwanted blue/green cast. - Match the color temperature of the light sources present in the scene — internal consistency between light sources and their rendered color is critical. - Neutral whites (fabric, walls, teeth, eyes) should remain neutral, not tinted by an incorrect global white balance shift. COLOR GRADING / MOOD: - Apply a subtle, professional cinematic color grade appropriate to the scene's intended mood. - Maintain skin tone accuracy as the top priority — never sacrifice natural skin tones for stylistic grading. - Avoid oversaturation; colors should feel rich but believable, not artificial or "AI-plastic." - Add subtle contrast (filmic S-curve) for depth, without crushing shadow or highlight detail. LIGHT QUALITY & CONSISTENCY: - Ensure all light sources in the frame cast physically consistent shadows and highlights in the same direction and intensity. - Soften any harsh, artificial-looking specular highlights on skin to match natural studio or ambient lighting. - Ensure rim light / background light does not overpower the key light on the subject's face. TEXTURE & REALISM: - Preserve natural skin texture, pores, and fabric weave — do not smooth or plasticize surfaces. - Retain fine detail in hair, fabric folds, and background elements; avoid AI-smoothing artifacts. - Slight natural film grain may be added for realism if the image appears too digitally "clean." OUTPUT: - The result should look like a professionally color-graded photograph straight out of a high-end camera, corrected by a colorist in post-production. - No change to composition, framing, subject pose, or background content — this is strictly a light and color correction pass. STRICT CONSTRAINTS: - Do not alter facial structure, identity, or proportions. - Do not add, remove, or reposition objects, people, or background elements. - Do not change the aspect ratio or crop. - Do not apply an artificial "filter" look. ``` ### Version express (retouche rapide) ``` Act as a professional colorist. Apply exposure and white balance correction only — do not change the subject's face, pose, clothing, or composition. - Fix exposure: recover blown-out highlights (skin, whites, light sources), lift crushed shadows, keep full natural detail. - Fix white balance: neutral, true-to-life skin tones; match color temperature to the visible light sources in the scene. - Add subtle cinematic contrast, no oversaturation, no plastic/AI-smooth skin. - Preserve natural skin texture, fabric detail, and a light natural grain. - Keep composition, framing, and aspect ratio identical to the original. Target mood: [warm golden-hour / neutral studio / cool natural daylight — choisir] Result must look like a real, professionally graded photograph — not filtered or artificial. ``` --- ## 3. RETOUCHE BEAUTÉ NATURELLE **Rôle : Réalisateur en maquillage de photo shooting, 20 ans d'expérience** ``` ROLE: You are acting as a professional on-set makeup artist and photo retoucher with 20+ years of experience in high-end photo shoots, specializing in natural, editorial-quality retouching that enhances without erasing identity. TASK: Apply a subtle, professional-grade beauty retouch to the subject in the image. The goal is polish and refinement — not transformation. The person must remain fully and immediately recognizable as themselves. SKIN: - Even out skin tone and reduce minor blemishes, redness, or uneven patches — but keep visible natural skin texture (pores, fine lines, natural variation). - Do not apply heavy smoothing, airbrushing, or plastic/AI-skin effect. - Preserve natural moles, freckles, scars, or distinctive marks unless they are temporary blemishes. - Reduce under-eye shadows or tiredness slightly, without erasing natural facial structure. - Keep natural shine/matte balance appropriate to skin type. FACE STRUCTURE: - Do not reshape the face, jawline, nose, eyes, lips, or any facial feature. - Do not slim, widen, or resize any part of the face or body. - Do not change age-related features beyond very light softening — the person's age should still read naturally. - Preserve asymmetries and unique facial characteristics exactly as in the original. EYES & BROWS: - Slightly enhance eye brightness and clarity without making eyes look unnatural or digitally enlarged. - Clean up stray brow hairs only if clearly a grooming correction, not a redesign of brow shape. HAIR: - Smooth obvious flyaways or stray strands if visually distracting, without changing hairstyle, hairline, volume, or color. - Preserve natural hair texture exactly as photographed. MAKEUP (only if requested / appropriate to context): - Enhance existing makeup subtly — do not add a full new makeup look unless explicitly asked. - Keep makeup intensity consistent with the mood of the original photo. CLOTHING & BODY: - Do not alter body shape, proportions, or posture. - Minor fixes only: smooth an obviously wrinkled or misplaced fabric fold if it's clearly a wardrobe malfunction. OVERALL FINISH: - The result should look like a subtle retouch done for a magazine editorial or professional portrait, not a beauty filter or face-tuning app effect. - If in doubt, retouch less rather than more. STRICT CONSTRAINTS: - Do not change the person's identity, facial recognition, or age in any noticeable way. - Do not resize, reshape, or "beautify" facial features beyond very light, natural correction. - Do not add or remove jewelry, accessories, or clothing items. - Do not change skin tone/complexion to a different shade. - Output must remain clearly and immediately identifiable as the same person. ``` ### Option événementielle Ajouter avant STRICT CONSTRAINTS : ``` Slightly richer, event-ready finish appropriate for a wedding/ceremony photo. ``` --- ## 4. CADRAGE & CHOIX D'OBJECTIF **Rôle : Cadreur cinéma, 25 ans d'expérience** ``` ROLE: You are acting as a professional cinematographer/camera operator with 25+ years of experience shooting feature films and high-end photo campaigns, with deep expertise in lens selection, framing, and depth of field to serve the emotional intent of each shot. TASK: Apply professional camera framing, lens characteristics, and depth of field to the image WITHOUT altering the subject's identity, facial features, pose, clothing, or the core content of the scene. This is a lens/composition pass only. LENS SELECTION: [choose one per scene — delete the others] — PORTRAIT LENS (85mm f/1.4 equivalent): Flattering compression on facial features, creamy soft background bokeh, subject sharply isolated from background, minimal background distortion, shallow depth of field with background elements softly melted into round, pleasing bokeh shapes. — ENVIRONMENTAL/GROUP LENS (35mm f/2 equivalent): Natural human-eye perspective, moderate depth of field keeping both subject and a good portion of the background in reasonable focus, ideal for full-body or group shots where the setting matters. — WIDE ESTABLISHING LENS (24mm f/2.8 equivalent): Broader field of view to show more of the environment/backdrop, subject remains the clear focal point despite the wider frame, no stretching or distortion of people near the edges of frame. — TELEPHOTO COMPRESSED LENS (135mm f/2 equivalent): Strong background compression, very shallow depth of field, subject isolated almost entirely from a creamy, abstracted background, ideal for tight emotional close-ups. FRAMING & COMPOSITION: - Apply classic compositional rules where appropriate: rule of thirds for subject placement, headroom appropriate to shot type, leading lines from the environment guiding the eye to the subject. - Maintain natural perspective — no unintended distortion of body or facial proportions from lens choice. - Ensure the subject remains the clear visual anchor of the frame. DEPTH OF FIELD & FOCUS: - Sharp, crisp focus on the subject's eyes/face. - Background softness should feel optically natural (true lens blur/bokeh), not a flat digital blur. - Foreground elements (if any) may have soft, artful blur consistent with the chosen lens's depth of field. CAMERA HEIGHT & ANGLE: - Default to eye-level camera height for a natural, flattering, connecting perspective, unless a specific emotional effect is desired. - Keep vertical lines (walls, drapery, doorframes) naturally straight unless an intentional dramatic tilt is requested. OUTPUT: - The result should read as a single photograph captured in-camera with the specified lens — authentic optical characteristics, not a digital filter simulating blur. - Preserve the full realism, texture, and lighting already established in the image. STRICT CONSTRAINTS: - Do not change the subject's identity, facial structure, proportions, pose, or clothing. - Do not alter the background content, only its optical rendering (sharpness/blur) as dictated by the chosen lens. - Do not distort bodies or faces at the edges of the frame. - Do not crop out or add new elements to the scene. - Output must remain photorealistic and physically plausible for the chosen lens and framing. ``` ### Guide de choix rapide | Type de scène | Objectif recommandé | |---|---| | Portrait serré (visage/buste) | 85mm | | Couple / duo en pied (ex : fond fleuri) | 35mm | | Décor large, ambiance événementielle | 24mm | | Plan intime très détouré du fond | 135mm | --- ## ORDRE D'UTILISATION RECOMMANDÉ Pour une photo générée de A à Z, applique les prompts dans cet ordre : 1. **Changement de fond** (§1) — poser le décor 2. **Cadrage & objectif** (§4) — définir la perspective et la profondeur de champ 3. **Exposition / balance des blancs** (§2) — corriger la lumière et la colorimétrie 4. **Retouche beauté** (§3) — finition sur le sujet --- *Document préparé pour Christ Media — Christian, Lomé, Togo.*
